# Europe evaluation additions

Apply the shared fit framework, then add these gates:

1. **Location gate:** exclude roles outside the selected countries or allowed remote geography.
2. **Authorization gate:** distinguish confirmed eligible, sponsorship explicitly offered, sponsorship unclear, and not eligible.
3. **Language gate:** separate working-language preference from a stated mandatory level. Do not auto-exclude an ambiguous requirement.
4. **Contract gate:** flag fixed-term, grant-funded, contractor, and permanent roles separately.
5. **Compensation normalization:** retain the original gross amount, period, currency, and stated benefits before making any comparison.
6. **Mobility cost:** flag relocation, travel, and on-site frequency rather than hiding them inside the fit score.
