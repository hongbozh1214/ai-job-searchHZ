# Finland search sources and query patterns

## Source order

1. Direct employer and university career pages; installed company-ATS skills.
2. Job Market Finland (`tyomarkkinatori.fi`) and Work in Finland (`workinfinland.com`).
3. EURAXESS and Academic Positions for research and university roles.
4. LinkedIn, Duunitori, and Jobly for broader coverage.
5. `valtiolle.fi` for central government and `kuntarekry.fi` for municipal/wellbeing-services roles when relevant.
6. Targeted searches of Finnish universities, research institutes, hospitals, biobanks, pharma, diagnostics, health-tech, and genomics employers.

## Query patterns

Use English and Finnish synonyms when appropriate:

```text
site:tyomarkkinatori.fi (bioinformatics OR bioinformatiikka OR genomics OR genomiikka)
site:workinfinland.com ("data scientist" OR bioinformatics OR genomics)
site:euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs Finland (bioinformatics OR genetics OR genomics)
site:academicpositions.com Finland (bioinformatics OR computational biology)
site:duunitori.fi (bioinformatiikka OR genomiikka OR "data scientist")
site:jobly.fi (bioinformatics OR biostatistics OR genetics)
```

Prefer the employer's original posting when an aggregator duplicates it.
