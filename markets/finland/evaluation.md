# Finland evaluation additions

Apply the shared fit framework, then add:

1. **Language:** distinguish mandatory Finnish/Swedish from preferred language and from advertisement language.
2. **Location:** record municipality, expected office days, commute feasibility, and relocation need.
3. **Contract:** flag fixed-term funding, trial period wording, collective-agreement references, and permanent roles.
4. **Eligibility:** record work-permit or residence-status implications only when supported; never infer sponsorship.
5. **Compensation:** preserve gross monthly salary, salary range, collective-agreement level, holiday pay, and benefits separately when stated.
6. **Public/academic evidence:** capture required degree, publication record, teaching/supervision, grant experience, and named application attachments.
7. **Application channel:** note whether the employer requires its portal, an application form, email, or academic attachments; drafting never equals submission.
