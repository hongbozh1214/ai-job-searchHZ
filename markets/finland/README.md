# Finland market

Use this overlay for roles based in Finland or explicitly remote from Finland. Keep Finnish, Swedish, and English language requirements distinct.

Before searching, resolve preferred Finnish regions, commute/relocation limits, remote/hybrid/on-site preference, work authorization, and whether Finnish- or Swedish-required roles should be included as stretch roles.

For research and life-science candidates, search both academic and industry titles. Finnish employers may advertise related work under `researcher`, `senior researcher`, `data scientist`, `bioinformatician`, `computational biologist`, `statistical geneticist`, `genomics scientist`, `biostatistician`, or `research coordinator`.
